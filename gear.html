Trailer Road Safety & Spares Box

Road Safety Mandates:
  • Heel Ties: Maximum 70 mm (7 cm) lift on all boat shoes (Rowing NZ safety rule).
  • Overhang: Maximum 7.0 m overhang from rear axle. Fluorescent red/yellow/orange flag (min 400x300mm) affixed to the longest hull.
  • Jockey Wheel: Completely wound up and turned 90° clear of the road surface before coupling lock.
  • Strapping: Straps must pull vertically down to rack crossbars (no angled tying). Bows pointing to the rear.
